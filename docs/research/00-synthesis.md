# Research Synthesis: Design Decisions for Keel

**Date:** 2026-06-07
**Inputs:** Six literature-review tracks in this directory (a through f), each with full citations. This document consolidates them into binding design deltas against the architecture spec (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-06-07-keel-kernel-architecture-design.md`).

## Track conclusions in one paragraph each

**(A) Robust numerics.** The exact-vs-tolerant war ended in synthesis: production-correct kernels use exact filtered predicates for every combinatorial decision (the EPICK discipline: never branch on a raw f64 sign) while keeping constructions inexact and tolerance-managed. Shewchuk's adaptive predicates remain the spine; Attene's indirect predicates (2020) extend exactness to constructed points (line-plane, three-plane intersections) at near-float speed, but only for linear primitives. No exact paradigm exists for general NURBS intersection, which is why tolerant topology must carry the irreducible uncertainty. Tolerance propagation algorithms are proprietary and underdocumented; Keel does original engineering there.

**(B) NURBS and freeform.** Evaluation is settled (de Boor/de Casteljau, per-span Bezier extraction for stability, never the power basis). The transformative architectural idea is IRIT's: one Bernstein-basis subdivision multivariate solver (Projected Polyhedron with Mourrain-Pavone reduction) behind every geometric query, with Bezier clipping as the quadratic local refiner and Yuksel/Blinn solvers for fixed low degrees. Trimmed-NURBS watertightness is intrinsically unachievable geometrically; topology must enforce it, which validates our tolerant B-rep stance. IGA hardened the refinement toolkit (knot insertion, Bezier extraction, smoothness-aware quadrature) and we reuse it.

**(C) Surface-surface intersection.** The geometry of SSI (locating points to tolerance) is solved; the topology of SSI (component count, small loops, tangencies, overlaps) is the actual hard problem and the dominant boolean failure source. The consensus architecture is topology-first: normal-cone loop-detection gate (Sederberg-Meyers, Hohmeyer), Interval Projected Polyhedron start points, then certified predictor-corrector marching (Ye-Maekawa differential geometry, per-step Krawczyk validation). Quadric-quadric gets the exact QI parameterization (Dupont et al.), not Levin's fragile pencil method. Overlap/coincidence extraction is a first-class path, not an afterthought. The Yang-Jia-Yan line (2023-2026) is the current state of the art and beats SISL/OCCT/ACIS on near-critical cases; track it closely.

**(D) Booleans and tolerant modeling.** The 2016-2023 mesh-boolean renaissance (Mesh Arrangements, indirect predicates, EMBER, Manifold) won by exactness, but its exactness does not transfer to NURBS-bounded solids whose intersection curves have no exact representation. What does transfer: winding-number-vector classification (more robust than neighborhood walking, gives n-ary booleans free), local imprinting along intersection curves only (no global arrangement), symbolic perturbation for degeneracies, and the coincidence-first mindset (exact touching is the common case in CAD, not the rare one). Per-entity local tolerances with propagation (Jackson 1995; epsilon-solidity, Qi/Shapiro 2006) must exist from day one; every source agrees retrofitting is a known dead end.

**(E) Kernel architecture.** Every successful kernel descends from Braid's BUILD; the differentiator is engineering discipline, not secret algorithms. Parasolid's lessons: tolerant modeling early, a small stable procedural API, decades of file compatibility, session/rollback transactions, and a colossal regression corpus (C3D cites 500k+ models) as the true robustness engine. OCCT's lesson in reverse: construction-time invariant enforcement beats post-hoc healing; its own docs concede booleans are "fundamentally not robust." Fornjot's retrospective pinpoints geometry/topology coupling and booleans as the killers and rejects SDF for precision CAD. Verdict on our structure: arena/handle + coedge + Euler operators is correct; keep the radial cycle able to hold 3+ coedges so partial-entity non-manifold support stays an extension, not a rewrite.

**(F) ML for geometry.** No neural method approaches kernel precision (documented ceiling near 1e-3 of bounding box vs our 1e-9 to 1e-12 decisions); nothing learned goes on the correctness path, ever. The valuable contract is AlphaGeometry's: the network proposes, an exact engine disposes. Kernel-safe applications, ranked: ML-guided fuzzing against kernel invariants (pure upside), Newton seed prediction for projection/tracing (classical iteration certifies), degeneracy prediction for preemptive precision escalation, algorithm-portfolio selection, Bayesian optimization of thresholds against the regression corpus. The B-rep deep-learning stack (UV-Net, BRepNet, ABC dataset) matters at the import/healing boundary and as a test-corpus source, not in the core.

## Consolidated design deltas (binding)

1. **Predicate spine.** Three-tier cascade per predicate: semi-static f64 filter, interval fallback, exact expansion. EPICK discipline codebase-wide: no combinatorial branch on an unfiltered f64 sign. Indirect predicates for constructed linear points. Investigate extending the exact island to quadric constructions. (A, D)
2. **Tolerance model.** Per-entity local tolerances with propagation are the primary model from day one, with the session resolution as the floor/default only. Single global epsilon is explicitly rejected by every track that touched the subject. Topology enforces watertightness; geometry is never assumed exact. (A, B, C, D, E)
3. **One solver to rule the queries.** A central Bernstein-basis subdivision multivariate solver in the math layer backs intersection, projection, and interrogation, with Bezier clipping refinement and Yuksel/Blinn closed-degree solvers. Eliminates per-query ad-hoc numerics, concentrates the robustness investment. (B)
4. **SSI is topology-first.** Loop-detection gate before any marching; IPP start points; certified marching with per-step Krawczyk validation; exact QI for quadric pairs; overlap/coincidence extraction as a first-class result kind. Tangential and overlap cases are designed for at the start of M5, not deferred. (C)
5. **Boolean pipeline.** BVH broad phase, local imprint along intersection curves, winding-number-vector classification, tolerant stitch with gap covering. Symbolic perturbation for degeneracies. Caller-tunable fuzzy mode planned. Coincidence-first. (D)
6. **Topology future-proofing.** Radial cycle structured for 3+ coedges per edge; partial-entity structure is the designated non-manifold path if it becomes a goal. Investigate persistent/immutable arenas for Parasolid-style rollback. (E)
7. **The corpus is the product.** Build the regression/degeneracy stress corpus as a first-class artifact from M1 (ABC dataset and generated adversarial cases feed it). A public NURBS-boolean robustness benchmark does not exist; publishing one is both our QA engine and a community contribution. (D, E, F)
8. **ML policy.** ML as seeding, heuristics, test generation, and tuning only; classical certification on every output; nothing learned on the correctness path. Experiment queue (CLI-runnable, in dependency order with the roadmap): learned-mutator fuzzing once invariants exist (M3+), Newton seed network for projection (M2+), degeneracy predictor for precision escalation (M5+), SSI algorithm-portfolio selector (M5+), Bayesian threshold tuning against the corpus (M6+), learned root-count oracle (M2+). Each experiment's design lives in track F's report. (F)

## What changed in the spec

Deltas 1-8 were applied to the architecture spec as amendments to D2 (predicates and tolerances), D4/D6 (solver consolidation), the M5/M6 roadmap entries (SSI and boolean architectures), a new D7 (ML policy), and the risk register (corpus as mitigation). The spec remains the single binding document; this synthesis records the why and the trail to sources.
