**Date:** 2026-06-07
**Inputs:** The deep-dive research corpus produced after synthesis v1: `docs/research/nurbs/` (7 files, ~200 sources) and `docs/research/kernel/` (25 files, ~600 sources), produced in three rounds (kernel files 01-10, then 11-20 covering topics surfaced by round one, then 21-24 closing the remaining literature gaps). This document consolidates them into binding design deltas against the architecture spec (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-06-07-keel-kernel-architecture-design.md`), continuing the numbering from synthesis v1 (deltas 1-8). Headline findings with file pointers live in `docs/research/nurbs/00-index.md` (findings 1-11) and `docs/research/kernel/00-index.md` (findings 1-30).

## What the deep dive changed, in one paragraph

Synthesis v1 settled the numerics, the solver architecture, SSI, and the boolean pipeline. The deep dive's largest contribution is at the system layer: two contracts that cannot be retrofitted (lineage events and determinism) must be day-one kernel obligations, because every persistent-naming scheme from Kripac (1995) to Cascaval (2023) consumes per-entity lineage, and OnShape demonstrates that deterministic regeneration is what makes naming, caching, and collaboration tractable above the kernel. The second contribution is precision about boundaries: constraint solving, motion, parametric regeneration, and PMI authoring all stay above the kernel, but each imposes specific, now-enumerated obligations on it. The third is risk removal: the patent landscape is mapped (three active no-fly zones, everything classical is safe), the predicate strategy is tiered by algebraic degree instead of hand-waved, and several subsystems that looked like four libraries (fitting, comparison, interrogation, extension) collapsed into one engine each.

## Binding design deltas (continuing v1's numbering)

9. **Lineage is a kernel primitive.** Every mutating operation emits per-output-entity lineage records: operation id, operand entity ids, derivation kind (created, modified, split, merged, deleted). Every persistent-naming scheme in the literature is a consumer of exactly this record; FreeCAD's topological-naming crisis is what its absence looks like. The emission contract must be settled before the topology layer freezes (M3), even though no naming layer ships before post-M7. Attribute systems get split/merge/delete callbacks for the same reason. (kernel/07, 03)

10. **Determinism is an API promise.** Identical inputs produce identical outputs across platforms, sessions, and thread counts: FP contraction pinned off in predicate code (FMA fusion can make orientation predicates non-antisymmetric), no fast-math anywhere, correctly-rounded transcendentals where results feed decisions (system libm is the largest cross-platform hole), no iteration over address- or hash-ordered containers (Rust HashMap is randomized by design), deterministic parallel reduction orders, and exact double round-tripping in serialization (lossy decimal would rot naming and caching). Rust's defaults cover much of this; the residual traps are enumerated in kernel/14. (kernel/08, 14)

11. **Region joins the hierarchy; PES is the confirmed non-manifold path.** The entity hierarchy gains first-class regions (Body, Region, Shell, Face, Loop, Coedge, Edge, Vertex) per Parasolid's model. The partial entity structure (Lee & Lee 2001) is confirmed as the designated non-manifold core: radial-edge expressive power at roughly half the storage. ACIS's bolt-on cellular topology is the documented cautionary tale; OCCT's boolean engine openly refuses non-manifold results. The fin/coedge consensus across Parasolid, ACIS, OCCT, and STEP 10303-42 means the hierarchy needs no further invention. Note: the spec's D3 (wave-2 verdict V1) adopts the stronger form of this delta, a non-manifold-capable representation, operator vocabulary, and validator from the first commit, with manifold-semantics-first implementation order; that stronger form governs. (kernel/01, 02)

12. **Copy-on-write persistent topology, promoted from investigation to commitment.** Structural sharing buys four features at once: undo, atomic rollback of failed operations (a failed boolean must never corrupt the model, which D5 already requires), cheap journaling snapshots for bug reproduction, and cacheable intermediate bodies for regeneration. Parasolid's deltas and ACIS's bulletin boards are both approximations of what a persistent data structure gives natively. (kernel/07, 19)

13. **Predicate strategy is tiered by algebraic degree.** Linear: exact, filtered (already bound by delta 1). Circles and conics: exact is affordable via degree-2 algebraic number comparison. Quadric intersection curves: exact-when-cheap; QI shows the curve is rational whenever singular and needs one square root in the smooth case, so escalate only when the data demands it. Splines: certified-numeric topology (interval subdivision, Krawczyk steps) over tolerant geometry, with no pretense of exactness. The supporting cost reality: CAD models are degeneracy-dense by design (intentional tangencies and coincidences), which is precisely where floating-point filters fail and naive exact fallback costs 100x-1000x. No shipped kernel delivers the exact-topology-plus-tolerant-geometry hybrid; it is Keel's sharpest differentiation opportunity. (kernel/11)

14. **NURBS implementation refinements.** Store control points in homogeneous 4D and divide last; run non-rational algorithms throughout. Cap the supported working degree and document it: basis conditioning grows like p times 2^p with degree and is knot-independent, so per-span Bezier extraction is the stability path, not knot tuning. Adopt cubic hybrid clipping (order-4 convergence at simple roots) as the default univariate root engine over plain Bezier clipping. Represent fillets, pipes, and sweeps procedurally (spine plus radius plus trim data) with NURBS approximation as a cache: a constant-radius fillet is a trimmed canal surface. Treat lofting's knot-merge explosion with common-knot reduction and approximate skinning. (nurbs/01-05)

15. **Tessellation: each model edge is discretized exactly once.** Both adjacent faces consume the same edge polyline; watertightness becomes a property of the algorithm, not a post-process. Mesh in (u,v) under the surface metric (first fundamental form) so 3D tolerances (sag L <= sqrt(8 R delta), normal deviation, max edge) hold on the surface, not in parameter space. Per-face parallelism with deterministic ordering per delta 10. Facet-to-face/edge attribution is part of the output contract. (kernel/05)

16. **One orthogonal-distance fitting engine.** A single fitting core with pluggable objective (least-squares, minimum-zone/Chebyshev, maximum-inscribed, minimum-circumscribed, constrained least-squares), robustness mode (metrology mode forbids outlier rejection; reverse-engineering mode requires it), and constraint set (coaxiality, perpendicularity between simultaneously fitted primitives). It serves healing's simplify phase, canonical recovery, datum simulation for future PMI, and scan fitting. Shakarji's NIST reference algorithms are the L2 baseline; exact optimality certificates exist for roundness (Voronoi) and min-zone (contact alternation) and should be used to certify, not just compute. (kernel/23)

17. **Canonical geometry recovery as a kernel service.** A recognize-fit-certify-substitute pipeline: exact structural tests first (Piegl-Tiller weight/knot patterns), then closed-form differential classification (constant principal curvature patterns; Keel's input is a noise-free spline, removing the scan literature's dominant error source), then the linear line complex eigenproblem for axis recovery (one 6x6 fit classifies cylinder vs revolution vs helix), then a certified max-deviation gate deciding substitution. Substitution is topology-coordinated (neighboring faces converted to the same analytic and merged) per OCCT's design lesson, and emits lineage events per delta 9. Runs at import, after booleans, and before feature recognition. (kernel/24, 13)

18. **Surface extension is a bounded, validated shared service.** Delete-face healing, tweak re-intersection, and untrimming are all extend-and-reintersect-and-retrim; extension is their shared core. Extrapolation degrades super-linearly with reach and rational weights can go negative, so the service enforces reach limits, validates against folding, and prefers exact analytic continuation whenever the surface is recognizable (delta 17 feeds this). (kernel/13, 03)

19. **Local operations are tweak-clients; build the imprint machinery for reuse.** Parasolid and ACIS implement offset-face, move-face, taper, delete-face-heal, and blend-removal as clients of one primitive: change face surfaces and re-intersect adjacent faces. Delete-face is the exception: it cannot be decomposed and stays a dedicated primitive. OCCT has no real tweak, which makes this the clearest open-source differentiation target after robust booleans. Consequence now: M5/M6 intersection and imprint components are designed as reusable services, not boolean-private code. (kernel/03)

20. **The comparison cascade is the testing oracle.** Cheap to expensive: entity counts, then deterministic mass properties (divergence-theorem integration with smoothness-aware quadrature), then rigid-invariant fingerprints (inertia eigenvalues, D2 distributions), then registration plus sampled Hausdorff/cloud-of-points, then entity correspondence. Implement CAx-IF/LOTAR validation properties natively: that one standard provides the oracle's cheap stages and interoperability with every commercial validator. (kernel/22, 08)

21. **License and patent policy.** Dual license MIT OR Apache-2.0 for the explicit patent grant. Three active no-fly zones, all with documented safe alternatives: automatic constraint inference during direct edits (Siemens, active to ~2034; ship direct modeling with explicit user-applied constraints instead), mixed facet/B-rep faces in a single body (convergent modeling, ~2035+; keep representations in separate bodies and convert at boundaries), and U-spline-class refinement (~2037; THB-splines are the safe choice, and the original T-spline patent expired March 2024). The entire classical core (NURBS, Euler operators, booleans, classic blends including setback and variable-radius, tessellation) rests on expired or never-patented art. (kernel/18)

22. **Boundaries confirmed, with enumerated kernel obligations.** Above the kernel, by both industry precedent and the literature: 2D sketch and 3D constraint solving (D-Cubed ships beside Parasolid; the witness-configuration method is the recommended diagnosis core for any future solver, and the live B-rep is itself a valid witness), motion/kinematics (every engine consumes the same triple: mass properties, joint axis+type, collision geometry; the kernel additionally owes joint-axis extraction from face pairs and time-parameterized swept volumes), parametric regeneration (a scheduler over deterministic, lineage-reporting, snapshot-able, fail-soft kernel operations; parallel feature regeneration has essentially no literature and is a potential original contribution), and PMI authoring (the kernel owes persistent entity references, offset-zone generation, and delta-16 datum fitting; tolerance zones are offset geometry per Requicha). Inside the kernel: planar profile geometry (one arrangement-plus-winding-number engine serves region extraction, curved planar booleans, and offset cleanup), tessellation, and interrogation (silhouettes, draft, curvature extrema, and umbilics are all zero-set queries against the delta-3 central solver). (kernel/04, 12, 21, 19, 17, 06)

## Confirmations (research that validated existing spec decisions)

- The topology hierarchy and Euler-operator discipline (D3) match the four-way consensus of Parasolid, ACIS, OCCT, and STEP 10303-42; Mantyla's completeness result licenses building all higher operations from the small operator set. (kernel/01)
- Arena plus generational handles (D3) is independently the answer reached by Parasolid tags, Fornjot's object store, and Rust practice; it also sidesteps borrow-checker pain on cyclic topology. (kernel/08)
- Winding-number-vector classification for booleans (M6) is reinforced: generalized winding numbers are additionally the robust point-containment oracle on leaky geometry, with a BSD implementation in LLNL Axom to study. (kernel/09, nurbs/06)
- The corpus-as-product testing doctrine (delta 7) gains a concrete methodology template: fTetWild's run-over-all-of-Thingi10K pass-rate reporting, applied to ABC's one million B-reps. (kernel/08)
- Analytics-first geometry (D4) is reinforced from the patent angle (classical analytic intersections are patent-free) and the recovery angle (delta 17 exists precisely because analytic intent is valuable enough to reconstruct). (kernel/18, 24)

## Watch list (open research, no literature to review)

- Curved snap rounding: no settled theory exists. If Keel needs finite-precision curved arrangements, that is original research.
- Parallel feature regeneration and parallel booleans: documented literature gap; industrial speed comes from caching and pruning.
- Collaborative parametric editing (CRDT-for-CAD): nascent; OnShape's centralized determinism is the only proven pattern.
- Learning-based healing: nascent as of 2025-2026; re-check annually.
- Midsurfacing: commercially unsolved; if ever shipped, ship with explicit failure reporting.

## What changed in the spec

This synthesis was consolidated in parallel with the implementation branch's own wave-2 and wave-3 syntheses (`docs/research/01-synthesis-wave2.md`, `docs/research/02-synthesis-wave3.md`), which absorbed the same corpus and in several places adopted stronger forms: the spec's D2 carries the full curved-predicate tier specification (superset of delta 13), D3 adopts non-manifold-capable representation from the first commit (stronger than delta 11), D9 binds lineage, determinism, sessions, and the boundary obligations together (deltas 9, 10, 12, 22), and D10 records the patent posture with a defensive-publication rule (delta 21). Where this synthesis and the wave syntheses overlap, the spec text derived from the waves governs. Contributions unique to this synthesis now in the spec: the cubic-hybrid-clipping default and homogeneous-4D/degree-cap notes in D6 (delta 14), the keel-tess and keel-io crate contracts (deltas 15 and 10's serialization clause), the comparison-cascade and pass-rate testing oracles (delta 20), the expanded post-M7 long tail with confirmed tier boundaries (deltas 16-19, 22), the patent and determinism entries in the risk register, and the cross-compiler differential-CI clause in D9. Round three (kernel/21-24) underpins deltas 16, 17, 20, and 22's motion obligations and has no wave counterpart. The spec remains the single binding document; this synthesis records the why and the trail to sources.
